# 2026-09-06 — the phone becomes a node, and three fixes it took to get there

## What changed

The dama phones now emit the Tier-1 sketch. A phone does not need to compress anything — it
holds an 8 s ring and a fat link. The **nodes** do, so the 172-byte log-mel sketch is what a
node's evidence looks like, and a classifier trained on node sketches only transfers to phone
events if the phone produces the same representation. The phones are the only sensors carrying
operator labels. Emitting the node's format is what makes them the nodes' training corpus rather
than a second, incompatible dataset.

`dama/<node>/acoustic_sketch`, one message per `audio_trigger` onset, joined on
`trigger_ts_utc_ms`. It is deliberately **not** folded into `audio_trigger`: that stream feeds
server-side TDOA and must not wait ~103 ms for post-onset audio the ring has not captured yet.

## ⚠️ Three defects the port exposed

**1. The wire frame could not state its own band edges.** `mel_filterbank` clamps its top edge to
Nyquist, so 20 bands span 300 Hz–20 kHz at 48 kHz and 300 Hz–7.84 kHz at 16 kHz. Band 12 is
4.6 kHz on one and 1.9 kHz on the other. The header carried `bands` and `frames` and never `fs`,
so a phone frame and a node frame that meant entirely different things were byte-indistinguishable
— in the exact scenario the port exists to enable. Flags bits 8–11 now carry a rate code, still
172 B, and `hear/corpus.py:feature_matrix` **requires** the rate rather than inferring it.
Inference fails silently on the realistic corpus: mostly 48 kHz phones with a handful of 16 kHz
nodes, majority wins, minority stacked onto an axis it was never measured on.

**2. The onset had to be re-derived, and numpy's `same` convolution is not centred.** The phone's
gate is chunk-granular (one 960-sample / 20 ms RMS comparison); a node's reports a sample index
from a 1 ms envelope. At a 4 ms hop, 20 ms is **five of the sketch's eight frames**. `SketchWindow`
re-derives the node's index from the same envelope. `np.convolve(..., "same")` keeps the middle
`len(x)` of the full convolution, which for an even kernel (48 taps at 48 kHz) is **not centred**:
`out[i]` averages `x[i-47+23 .. i+23]`. Centring it moves the argmax half a millisecond — 24
samples, ~8 m of apparent range. Pinned by golden vectors at 41 probe points per case.

**3. `unpack` did not validate.** It sliced the body and reshaped, so a truncated radio frame
surfaced as numpy's "cannot reshape array of size 157" three frames down the stack — and a body
short by a whole row would have reshaped **cleanly into the wrong geometry**. Found by the corpus
tests, not by reading it.

Verification is on a real JVM against the Python's vectors: 0 of 160 bytes differ across
impulse/tone/noise/silence/clipped at 48 kHz plus a 16 kHz case for the Nyquist clamp. Running
them is also what caught `@JvmStatic` missing — `AcousticSketch` is a Kotlin `object`, so the Java
test could not have compiled.

## The gate now has a control

`check_array` passed 8.3 % of 205 Kinect impulses and that number was published without a null.
Two nulls are now in the module:

| null | Kinect, tol 31.25 µs | what it measures |
|---|---|---|
| uniform decoy, per pair over its own \|τ\| ≤ d/c | **0.50 %** | additivity alone; bound and Nyquist disarmed by construction |
| misassociation (real impulses, wrong triplet) | **8.5 %** (field, 43/504) | the operational false-alarm rate |

So the gate discriminates **5:1**, not 16:1, and 8.5 % is the number to plan around. `interval_consistent`
has the same shape of problem in reverse — see below.

⚠️ Neither null sees a **coherent lie**. 26 phone events once showed \|τ\| ≤ 0.2 ms on every pair
with closure residual 0.00 ms and the gate said VALID: whole-spectrum PHAT promoting common-mode
filter ringing to a peak at lag zero. All-zero delays are perfectly additive. Only the band
restriction catches that one.

## The 21:04–21:05 burst: a negative result, and it is the one the report predicted

Crack–blast on the three phones (`analysis_20260905/cb_phones_2104.py`) needs no clock, no
bearing, no cross-node sync — two arrivals on one microphone — so it steps around every timing
failure of 2026-09-05. Against the **corrected** threshold (22 dB, FA ≤ 1 %; the old 12 dB figure
was referred to the pre-event floor and runs at ~41 % FA):

| burst | phones clearing 22 dB | at 20 dB (FA 1.9 %) |
|---|---|---|
| 1788642287.513 | 0 of 3 | 0 |
| 1788642292.525 | 0 of 3 | 0 |
| 1788642335.632 | **1** of 3 | 2 |

One range is a sphere, not a position. This is exactly the falsification the report named up
front: *"fewer than two phones showing a second arrival above the corrected null."* A phone
outside the Mach cone hears only the blast and yields no interval.

⚠️ **And the cross-phone bound cannot fail on this geometry.** `|dt_i − dt_j| ≤ 2d/c` is
58.7 / 75.4 / 115.9 ms at these separations against an onset scatter near 2 ms. Every pair passed
on every burst — *including* pairs whose implied ranges differ by a factor of two (41.2 ms against
91.6 ms on the same shot). A check that cannot fail is not evidence the picks are right. For it to
discriminate you need `2d/c` comparable to the scatter: **d ≲ 0.35 m**. That is a co-located pair
— what a Tier-1 node array is, and what surveyed flags tens of metres apart are not. It is a
direct argument for the node build over more phones.

## Elsewhere: the 5.128 h stamp error, root-caused

`hugbot5000/perception/audio_ring.py`. `AudioRing.create()` is `O_CREAT` without `O_TRUNC` and
`struct.pack_into` writes only the first 32 bytes — the 32 spare bytes holding the **timebase**
survived a re-create at the same path and size. `write_counter` went back to 0; the anchor did
not. Every frame of the new capture was then dated off the previous run's anchor, linearly and
silently. That is `~/shots_20260905_205209`, stamped 18 459 s wrong — the capture holding this
burst. Reproduced in four lines, fixed by zeroing the header, and `frame_time_ns` now refuses an
anchor that cannot be about frames the ring holds instead of extrapolating one forever.
